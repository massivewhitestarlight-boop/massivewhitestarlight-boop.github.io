What’s inside

Five ways to stop
cheating yourself.

Every feature asks for proof — a solved task, a counted rep, a phone left face down.

01

Alarm

Stops ringing only after you complete the tasks you set. No snooze-and-sleep.

02

Workouts

The camera counts your reps right on the iPhone. A rep you didn’t do doesn’t count.

03

Focus

Put the phone face down and get to work. Pick it up — and the session knows.

04

Food diary

Scan a barcode or a label instead of typing everything by hand.

05

Habits

Goals and habits you back up with progress — including photos you take yourself.
