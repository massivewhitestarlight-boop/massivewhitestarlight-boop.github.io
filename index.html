Drop by drop

Six sections.
One rule: prove it.

Every section asks for proof — a solved task, a counted rep, a phone left face down, a photo of your progress.

01

Alarm

Stops ringing only after the wake-up tasks you choose: math, a QR code, a tile sequence, a typed phrase. Backup alarms if you try to sleep through.

02

Workouts

Squats, push-ups, pull-ups, crunches, jumps and the plank. The camera counts reps and checks your form right on the iPhone.

03

Focus

Put the phone face down and the timer starts. Pick it up or leave the app — and the session knows.

04

Food diary

Scan a barcode, photograph the nutrition label or pick from the built-in food reference. Optional allergen warnings.

05

Habits & goals

Habits to build and habits to quit, with reminders. Goals that count only with proof — like a photo taken right then.

06

Achievements

A month calendar for every section, streaks and badges. Every day you show up is one more drop.
