Common questions

Which iPhones are supported?

Discipline works on iPhones with iOS 26.1 or later.

The alarm didn’t ring. What should I check?
  1. Open iPhone Settings → Discipline and make sure Alarms and Notifications are allowed.
  2. Check that the alarm is turned on in the App and the time is correct.
  3. In the App, Settings → Alarm diagnostics shows which alarms are actually scheduled in the system.
Reps aren’t being counted.

Place the iPhone so your whole body is in the frame, with good lighting and a contrasting background. For crunches and the plank, set the phone to the side of you. Make sure the App has access to the camera in Settings → Discipline → Camera.

The scanned product wasn’t found.

Product data comes from the open database Open Food Facts, and not every product is in it yet. Photograph the nutrition label instead — the App reads it on your device — or add the entry manually. For unpackaged foods like fruit, grains or meat, use the built-in food reference.

Why does the App ask me to photograph the ingredients?

If you’ve chosen allergens to be warned about, the App asks for a photo of the ingredient list once per product, because databases don’t always list allergens. The App remembers the result for that product. Warnings are an aid, not a guarantee — always check the packaging if you have an allergy.

Do I need an account or internet?

No. Discipline works without an account and without internet. The connection is used only to look up a scanned barcode in Open Food Facts.

Where is my data stored? Can I transfer it to a new iPhone?

All data is stored only on your iPhone — the App doesn’t send it anywhere and doesn’t use iCloud sync. When moving to a new iPhone, data may be transferred as part of a regular iPhone backup or device-to-device transfer.

How do I delete all my data?

In the App, open Settings → Erase all history. Deleting the App from your iPhone also removes all its data from the device. I don’t store any copy of your data.

How do I change the language or theme?

In the App, open Settings: there you can choose the language (English or Russian) and a light, dark or system theme.

Reporting a bug

To help me fix a problem faster, please include:

  • your iPhone model and iOS version;
  • the App version (Settings → Version in the App);
  • what you did, what you expected, and what happened;
  • a screenshot or screen recording, if possible.

Your email address is used only to reply to you. See the Privacy Policy.
