In short

Discipline does not collect, store or share your personal data.

  • No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no purchases.
  • All your data is stored only on your iPhone.
  • Camera images are processed on the device and never sent anywhere.
  • The only network request: a scanned barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts to find the product.

Who we are

This Privacy Policy describes how the Discipline app for iPhone (“the App”) handles your information. The App is developed and published by Dmitry Kukhar, an independent developer (“I”, “me”). If you have any questions, contact me at massivewhitestarlight@gmail.com.

Information I collect

I do not collect any personal data. The App has no user accounts, no registration, no analytics or tracking SDKs, no advertising, and no in-app purchases. I do not operate any server that receives your data, and I have no access to anything you enter in the App.

The App does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies and does not share data with data brokers.

Data stored on your device

Everything you create in the App is stored locally on your iPhone and never sent to me. This includes:

  • alarms and their tasks;
  • habits and goals;
  • food diary entries;
  • focus sessions;
  • “Thoughts before sleep” notes;
  • photos you attach to goals.

The App does not use iCloud or CloudKit. Please note that if you have enabled iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, iOS may include the App’s data in that backup. Such backups are managed by Apple and governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy, not by me.

Camera

The App uses the camera to count exercise repetitions, scan barcodes and QR codes, and read food labels. All camera images are processed on your device in real time. Video and frames used for these features are not saved and are not transmitted anywhere.

The only images the App stores are the photos you deliberately take for your goals. They remain on your device.

Barcode lookup (Open Food Facts)

This is the only time the App connects to the internet. When you scan a product barcode, the App sends only the barcode number to the open database Open Food Facts to retrieve product information. No other information from the App — your diary, name, photos or identifiers — is sent.

As with any internet request, Open Food Facts’ servers receive technical connection data such as your IP address. Their handling of this data is described in the Open Food Facts Privacy Policy. If you prefer, you can add food entries manually without scanning.

Permissions

The App may ask for the following permissions. Each is used only for the purpose described and can be changed at any time in iPhone Settings.

PermissionWhy it’s needed
CameraCounting reps, scanning barcodes and QR codes, reading labels, taking goal photos. Processed on device.
NotificationsReminders and alerts from the App.
Alarms (AlarmKit)Scheduling alarms that ring until you complete their tasks.
Live ActivitiesShowing a status banner on the Lock Screen, for example during a focus session.

Motion sensors (used, for example, for shake gestures and “face-down” focus) do not require a separate permission. Their data is processed on your device only and is not stored or transmitted.

Sharing and selling

I do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with anyone, because I do not collect it in the first place. The App contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking code.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device for as long as you keep it in the App. You can delete individual entries inside the App. Deleting the App from your iPhone permanently removes all of its data from the device (except any copies that may exist in your own device backups).

Children

The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because no data is collected, there is no personal information to review or delete on my side.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in the EU or similar laws elsewhere), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Since I do not collect or store your personal data, all of your data is already under your control on your device. If you have any request or question, write to massivewhitestarlight@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If the way the App handles data changes, I will update this page and the “Last updated” date above. Significant changes will be reflected in the App Store listing before they take effect.
