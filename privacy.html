In short

Discipline does not collect, store or share your personal data.

  • No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no purchases.
  • All your data is stored only on your iPhone.
  • Camera images are processed on the device and never sent anywhere.
  • The only network request: a scanned barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts to find the product.

About this policy

This Privacy Policy describes how the Discipline: Drop by Drop app for iPhone (“Discipline”, “the App”) handles your information. The App is created by an independent developer (“I”, “me”). If you have any questions, contact me at massivewhitestarlight@gmail.com.

Information I collect

I do not collect any personal data. The App has no user accounts, no registration, no analytics or tracking SDKs, no advertising, and no in-app purchases. I do not operate any server that receives your data, and I have no access to anything you enter in the App.

The App does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies and does not share data with data brokers.

Data stored on your device

Everything you create in the App is stored locally on your iPhone and never sent to me. This includes:

  • alarms, their wake-up tasks and settings;
  • workout history;
  • focus sessions;
  • food diary entries, your own dishes and the allergens you choose to be warned about;
  • habits, goals and the photos you take for your goals;
  • “Thoughts before sleep” notes;
  • achievements, badges and the in-app event log;
  • app settings such as theme, language and reminders.

The App does not use iCloud or CloudKit. Please note that if you have enabled iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, iOS may include the App’s data in that backup. Such backups are managed by Apple and governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy, not by me.

Camera and photos

The App uses the camera to count exercise repetitions and check your form, scan barcodes and QR codes, read nutrition labels and ingredient lists, and take photos for your goals. All camera images are processed on your device in real time. Video and frames used for counting and scanning are not saved and are not transmitted anywhere. Photos of labels and ingredient lists are used only to read the text on your device and are not saved.

If you choose a photo of a label from your photo library, iOS gives the App access only to the photo you pick; the App does not get access to the rest of your library.

The only images the App stores are the photos you deliberately take for your goals. They remain on your device and are deleted together with the goal.

Food data sources

Open Food Facts (online)

This is the only time the App connects to the internet. When you scan a product barcode, the App sends only the barcode number to the open database Open Food Facts to retrieve product information. No other information from the App — your diary, name, photos or identifiers — is sent.

As with any internet request, Open Food Facts’ servers receive technical connection data such as your IP address. Their handling of this data is described in the Open Food Facts Privacy Policy. If you prefer, you can add food without scanning: by photographing the label, from the built-in reference, or manually.

Built-in food reference (offline)

For unpackaged foods such as fruit, vegetables, grains or meat, the App includes a reference of average nutrition values, mostly based on USDA FoodData Central, the open database of the U.S. Department of Agriculture. This reference is built into the App and works offline: searching it does not send anything anywhere.

Allergen warnings

Allergen warnings are an aid, not a guarantee: product databases and label reading can be incomplete or wrong. Always check the packaging yourself if you have an allergy.

Permissions

The App may ask for the following permissions. Each is used only for the purpose described and can be changed at any time in iPhone Settings.

PermissionWhy it’s needed
CameraCounting reps, scanning barcodes and QR codes, reading labels, taking goal photos. Processed on device.
NotificationsReminders for habits and focus, the evening reminder and other alerts from the App.
Alarms (AlarmKit)Scheduling alarms that ring until you complete their tasks.
Live ActivitiesShowing a status on the Lock Screen, for example during a focus session.

Motion sensors (used for shake gestures and “face-down” focus) do not require a separate permission. Their data is processed on your device only and is not stored or transmitted.

During a focus session the App checks only whether a phone call is in progress, so that answering a call does not count as leaving the session. The App does not learn who is calling and does not access your contacts or call history.

Sharing and selling

I do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with anyone, because I do not collect it in the first place. The App contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking code.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device for as long as you keep it in the App. You can delete individual entries, habits and goals inside the App, or erase all history at once in Settings → Erase all history. Deleting the App from your iPhone permanently removes all of its data from the device (except any copies that may exist in your own device backups).

Children

The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because no data is collected, there is no personal information to review or delete on my side.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in the EU or similar laws elsewhere), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Since I do not collect or store your personal data, all of your data is already under your control on your device. If you have any request or question, write to massivewhitestarlight@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If the way the App handles data changes, I will update this page and the “Last updated” date above. Significant changes will be reflected in the App Store listing before they take effect.
